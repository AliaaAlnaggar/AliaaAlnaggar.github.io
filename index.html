Hi, I'm Dr. Aliaa Alnaggar

I am an Assistant Professor in the Industrial and Systems Engineering Department at Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT)[cite: 1]. Prior to joining RIT, I was an Assistant Professor in Industrial Engineering at Toronto Metropolitan University (TMU) and an affiliate of the Data Science and Analytics Program[cite: 1]. I hold a Ph.D. and MASc in Management Sciences from the University of Waterloo, co-supervised by Prof. Fatma Gzara and Prof. Jim Bookbinder[cite: 1]. I was also a Postdoctoral Fellow in Operations Management at the Rotman School of Management, University of Toronto, collaborated with Prof. Andre Cire and Prof. Adam Diamant, and was awarded the NSERC Postdoctoral Fellowship[cite: 1].

My research focuses on leveraging operations research and analytics techniques to optimize service design and operations in uncertain environments, with particular emphasis on crowdsourced delivery, the sharing economy, healthcare systems, transportation, and sustainability[cite: 1]. My methodological expertise includes distributionally robust optimization, stochastic programming, dynamic programming, Markov decision processes, reinforcement learning, and machine learning integration into optimization frameworks[cite: 1].
